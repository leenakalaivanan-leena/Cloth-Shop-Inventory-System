Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing clothing shop inventory, sales, profit, stock availability, and supplier performance using Power BI.

Objectives
Monitor sales and profit performance.
Track available stock and quantity sold.
Identify low-stock products.
Analyze store and product category performance.
Evaluate supplier performance.

Data Source

The project data is stored and queried using Snowflake SQL. Data is exported for use in Excel and Power BI.

Key Insights
Total sales of ₹1.13M with a profit of ₹283.4K and a profit margin of 25%.
Sarees are the top-selling category, followed by Winter Wear and Dresses.
Women's products contribute about 60% of total sales; Men's products about 40%.
Pune Store has the highest sales among the 10 stores.
26 items are low on stock and need replenishment.
Sri Garments is the best-performing supplier.
